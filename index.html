    bite score by day

    Score blends moving tide, solunar feeding times, moon phase, dawn/dusk and wind. A rule of thumb, not a promise.

    What do these words mean?
    Bite score
    0 to 100. Higher means better odds right now: moving water, a feeding time, dawn or dusk, and a calm enough wind.
    Feeding time (solunar)
    Times when the moon is overhead, underfoot, rising or setting. Fish tend to feed more then. Majors last about 2 hours, minors about 1.
    Rising / falling tide
    Moving water carries bait and turns fish on. Slack tide (not moving) is usually slow.
    Slot
    A size range. You can keep a fish only if it's inside it, e.g. redfish 18 to 27 inches.
    Total vs fork length
    Total: nose to tail tip, tail squeezed. Fork: nose to the middle of the tail fork.
    Bag limit
    How many you may keep per person per day.
    Moon names
    Gibbous means more than half lit, crescent less. Waxing is growing toward full, waning is shrinking. Full and new moons bring the biggest tides.

    Do I need a license?

    If you cast a line in salt water, even catch and release, you need one unless you're exempt.

    Under 16
    No license or snook permit needed.
    Florida resident 65+
    No license needed. Carry your Florida driver's license or ID.
    Shore, bridge, seawall or pier fixed to land
    Florida residents can get the free Resident Shoreline license. It does not cover fishing from a boat or kayak, or a shore you reached by boat.
    Boat, kayak, or any visitor 16+
    Saltwater license: residents $17 a year or $79 for 5 years. Visitors $17 for 3 days, $30 for 7 days, $47 a year.
    Charter, party boat or licensed pier
    You're covered by their license. Ask to be sure.

    Extra permits

    Snook
    $10 a year on top of your license to keep a snook, shore fishing included.
    Sharks from shore
    Free shore-based shark permit after a short online course. Needed for sharks from beaches, bridges and piers, even if you're otherwise exempt.
    Reef fish from a private boat
    Free State Reef Fish Angler designation for grouper, snapper, hogfish and other reef fish.
